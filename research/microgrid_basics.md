# Microgrid Fundamentals

Microgrid - A standalone electricity distribution network that is decentralised (independent from the main power grid) and relies on local energy production.

Microgrids are powered by generators or renewable energy sources, and generally include an energy storage unit.
- At the crux of these microgrids is a central controls software system that will optimise energy storage and production based on demand.
- Hardware to store and distribute the energy produced
- 100 kW to several megawatts of power generated


Power Generated = Power Consumed + Power Stored
